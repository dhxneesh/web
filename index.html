Smart Glove for Assembly Verification

Final-year project, NTU

A glove prototype for quality control in automotive manufacturing, built after proof-of-concept studies on electrical wiring assembly. It detects connector mating events in real time from synchronised audio and force signals.

  • STM32 firmware in C (STM32CubeIDE) acquiring synchronised audio and force data with DMA and ADC
  • Real-time acquisition pipelines for audio and force event detection, synchronised by clock rate
  • Boolean AND-gate sensor fusion logic for connector verification, built from analysis of the time-aligned signals
  • STM32
  • C
  • Sensor fusion
  • Signal processing
